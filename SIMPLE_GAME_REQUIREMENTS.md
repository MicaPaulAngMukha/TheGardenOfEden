# Garden of Eden - Game Requirements

## Player Requirements:
- The player must be able to start the game
- The player must be allowed to move the character using WASD or arrow keys
- The player must be allowed to interact with objects using E or Space
- The player must experience damage and lose lives when hit by enemies
- The player must be allowed to play through 6 levels sequentially
- The player must be allowed to retry after death
- The player's held key must be saved across levels

## Game Requirements:
- The game must be allowed to be paused using Escape
- The game must be allowed to toggle sounds
- The game must save the player's held key between levels
- The game must display the player's remaining lives (hearts)
- The game must display the held key above the player
- The game must be allowed to play using keyboard controls (WASD/Arrows)
- The game must spawn 4 different keys after Mikhail's chase begins
- After death, the game must be allowed to retry or quit
- The game must run at 60 FPS

## Character Requirements:
- The player must be allowed to move in 4 directions
- The player must be allowed to pick up keys from bushes
- The player must be allowed to swap keys by picking up a different key
- The player must be allowed to refuse key pickups
- Dropped keys must remain visible on the ground
- The player must wait 3 seconds before re-picking up a dropped key
- The player must die upon losing all lives
- Mikhail must chase the player after 3 encounters
- Raziel must provide hints about the keys
- The Naga must chase the player through a maze
- Cain must throw spears at the player
- Abel must chase and stun the player
- The Guardian must patrol in darkness and chase during light
- God must spawn in 3 levels when player has Divine key
- God must display special spawn dialogue after intro
- God must spawn lightning bolts and can instant-kill the player

## Difficulty Requirements:
- Bronze Key (Easy) must reduce enemy speed and increase item spawns
- Gold Key (Normal) must use default difficulty
- Rusted Key (Hard) must increase enemy speed and reduce item spawns
- Divine Key (God Mode) must spawn God entity and apply extreme difficulty
- The game must hide the difficulty indicator to keep it a surprise
- The game must apply different modifiers based on held key

## Level Requirements:
- Start level must introduce Mikhail and Raziel
- Start level must spawn 4 keys when chase begins
- TheNaga must have lever-controlled walls and maze layout
- TheTwins must require collecting fragments to banish each twin
- TheGuardian must have darkness/light cycles and hidden key
- TheStatues must have puzzle mechanics
- TheGarden must be the final level with Tree of Knowledge
- Each level must transition to the next upon completion
